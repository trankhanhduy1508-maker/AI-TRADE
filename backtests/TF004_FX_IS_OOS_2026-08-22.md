## Objective conclusion

TF-004 produced two positive and four negative OOS net results after the fixed
research cost proxy. The channel-trailing exit did not establish robust
performance across the tested FX symbols and intervals. Hourly trade counts
were materially higher than TF-003 because the declared 20-bar channel stop
frequently closed positions; that is an observed behavior, not a reason to
retune this experiment.

The result remains preliminary because the cost profile and Yahoo research feed
are not broker-verified, the sample is limited, and no capital-risk accounting
was used. It is not live or MT5-demo evidence, and it does not validate or
invalidate Trend Following generally. No parameter was changed after the run.

## Next action

Keep TF-004 as a fixed, documented comparison. Before any MT5 expansion, obtain
broker/demo data and cost evidence, then test execution/recovery infrastructure
independently of strategy performance.
