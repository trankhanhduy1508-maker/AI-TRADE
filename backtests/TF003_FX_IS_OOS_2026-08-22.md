## Objective conclusion

TF-003 produced mixed preliminary OOS results: three of six tested symbol/
interval combinations were positive after the declared research cost proxy,
and three were negative. The same fixed implementation did not show a uniform
pass across markets and timeframes. This is not evidence of live profitability,
not a broker-data result, and not evidence that the general Trend Following
principle is false.

The result remains preliminary because the cost profile and Yahoo research feed
are not broker-verified, the sample is limited, and the current engine uses a
fixed target rather than a channel/trailing exit. No parameter was changed
after observing the result. TF-003 therefore does not pass the gate for MT5
demo expansion yet.

## Next action

Preserve TF-003 as a fixed comparison baseline. Improve evidence quality with a
lawful broker/demo data path and separately specify a trend-following exit
model that lets the exit rule be tested without silently changing this result.
