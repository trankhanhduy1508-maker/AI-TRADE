## Objective conclusion

The fixed TF-001 implementation did not produce a positive OOS net result on
the tested hourly samples after the declared research cost proxy. Daily samples
produced no qualifying trades. This does not prove the general Trend Following
principle false, and it is not a broker/live result; it means this specific
implementation, source, period, and unverified cost profile have not passed the
current validation gate.

The result is recorded as preliminary because the cost profile is not broker
verified and the downloaded feed is a research source rather than an MT5 broker
feed. No parameters were changed after seeing the result.
