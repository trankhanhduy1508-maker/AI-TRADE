# TF-003 / TF-004 independent temporal holdout - 2026-08-22

## Scope and controls

This is a separate historical window used after the recent-period sensitivity
round. The six-variant grid was inherited unchanged; no parameters were
selected or adjusted using these results.

- Window: approximately 2016-08-21 through the closed bars before the
  2021-08-22 UTC cutoff.
- Markets: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, Gold (`GC=F`), BTC-USD and Oil
  (`CL=F`).
- Data: seven Yahoo daily research proxies, kept outside the repository;
  exact rows and hashes are recorded below.
- Costs: the same `UNVERIFIED` price-unit research profiles used in P23/P24.
- Evaluation: full fixed-period run for each of 6 variants x 7 markets = 42
  cases. This is a temporal holdout, not a broker or account return.
- R metrics use each trade's initial entry-to-stop risk.

The first filtered-artifact smoke run was discarded because PowerShell added a
UTF-8 BOM to the generated CSV header. The final files were rewritten as
UTF-8 without BOM, a smoke test passed, and all 42 cases were rerun.

## Analysis and learning

1. TF-003 does not survive this earlier regime as a general multi-asset
   candidate: all three RR variants are negative in aggregate and positive in
   at most one market.
2. TF-004 channel 10 has a large positive aggregate, but EURUSD and GBPUSD
   contribute 206.463R while Gold and AUDUSD contribute -39.288R. The result
   is concentrated and therefore not a portfolio-quality robustness claim.
3. TF-004 channel 20 is positive in aggregate only because BTC contributes
   67.666R; the other six markets sum to -39.821R. Channel 40 is similarly
   dominated by BTC (+80.642R), with EURUSD and GBPUSD negative.
4. The market concentration differs from the recent walk-forward evidence,
   where channel 10 was dominated by USDJPY and channel 20 had different
   winners. This regime/market instability is evidence against promoting a
   single universal market set or timeframe without further validation.
5. Win rates near or above 30% do not rescue a concentrated result. The
   acceptance requirement remains positive expectancy/net R, controlled
   drawdown, and stability across time, markets, costs and execution
   contracts.

## Gate decision

The independent holdout is **RESEARCH / UNVERIFIED**. It does not authorize a
canonical strategy, MT5 demo forward trading, or live trading. No strategy
rule, RR/channel parameter, hard risk limit, or execution permission was
changed after this holdout.

Next work remains broker-aligned contract/cost validation, then a controlled
MT5 demo harness only after the IPC boundary is operational. Live-money
trading remains locked.
