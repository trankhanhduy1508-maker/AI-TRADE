## Immutable R2 learning protocol
- Freeze before inspecting new empirical outcomes: default `FLAT` unless enough training evidence; 500 fully completed bars minimum for each D1/H4 episode, 250-bar step, at least 200-bar warm-up, rolling *past-only* training span 1,000 bars, minimum 5 completed training trades. No future-dated/unclosed candle.
- Frozen candidates: `R0_REFERENCE` channel 55, trailing 20, ATR multiple 2.5; `R2_FAST` channel 40, trailing 15, ATR multiple 2.0; `R2_SLOW` channel 80, trailing 30, ATR multiple 3.0. SMA200, ATR20 and next-bar-open/gap-worse execution remain frozen. These R2 candidates and selection rule are defined BEFORE first empirical run; this does NOT amend or rerun the R0 historical OOS for an R0 edge claim. Short allowed only if documented executable vehicle; cash indices are nontradeable proxies.
- At each boundary `T`, compute all candidate training metrics on *bars before T* and choose the candidate with largest training `expectancy_r - 0.01 * max_drawdown_r_marked`; require at least 5 closed training trades; deterministic ties resolved by lexicographically smallest candidate ID. If none eligible, `FLAT`. Freeze selection through next 250-bar chronological episode. Then incorporate newly observed evaluation episode into subsequent training pool; never revise earlier episode choices/outputs. Validation/evaluation episodes are historical descriptive prequential OOS, NOT independent new market data.
- For isolation use prefix only `bars[:T]` for training and `bars[:episode_end]` for evaluation; reset flat at episode start, do NOT assume carry across a model-switch boundary. Any position still open at episode end is recorded unrealized only, not a closed trade or secretly liquidated. Report this flatten/reset approximation. A later production execution engine must explicitly carry or liquidate with costs; do not promote episode PnL to account currency or live strategy.
- Report all symbols and unavailable cells, candidate selection by episode, sample size, closed/open trades, actual mean win/loss realized RR (not preset), expectancy R, adverse gap outcomes, marked DD, data gaps, baseline flat and provider-matched passive only when properly defined. Summing R or price PnL across heterogeneous symbols does NOT create a portfolio return.
